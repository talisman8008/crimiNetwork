STATEMENT OF COMPLAINANT
------------------------------------------------------------------------

The complainant, Hasmukh Mehta, a wholesale cloth merchant operating
from Dadar TT Circle, states that in the first week of April 2026, two
persons visited his shop and represented themselves as procurement agents
of a fictional shipping and logistics firm named Global Freight Services.

The first of these persons introduced himself as Imran, the second
as Rajan. They stated that their firm required a large bulk consignment
of cotton fabrics and requested samples and a proforma invoice. On their
second visit on 02/05/2026, all three accused arrived together in
vehicle no. MH-01-DT-1123. The third person, subsequently identified
as Vikram Naik, presented himself as the logistics coordinator.

The accused produced fictitious letterheads and documentation purporting
to be from Global Freight Services and persuaded the complainant to
transfer an advance payment of Rs. 3,50,000 via NEFT to a bank account
they represented as belonging to the firm. The consignment was never
delivered and the accused became unreachable thereafter.

Inquiries with the Registrar of Companies reveal no entity named
Global Freight Services registered at the address provided on the
letterhead. The bank account to which the transfer was made is stated
by the complainant's banker to have been opened in the name of
RK Enterprises, details of which are under investigation.

A cashier at the complainant's shop, Nasreen Shaikh W/o Farukh Shaikh
R/o Nagpada, Mumbai - 400008, Phone: +91-90000-66677, who was present
during the accused's visits, has provided a statement corroborating
the complainant's account and confirming the identities of the accused
from photographs shown to her.

Investigating officers note a possible organised criminal nexus between
the three accused, with coordinated roles observed across this FIR and
FIR Nos. 0112/2026 (Andheri PS) and 0187/2026 (Kurla PS).
